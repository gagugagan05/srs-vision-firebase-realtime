# SRS Vision v5 — Interaction Update

The main dashboard is fully interactive: KPI cards open their relevant dashboards; recent project rows open project editing; Recent Activity entries open their sections; Team Overview exposes Edit Team and Customise actions; work and assignment summaries open their live areas; tool slots open dedicated workspaces. Team member Edit/Customise buttons open their dialogs immediately. Nested action controls are protected from ancestor navigation so Start/Share/Edit buttons perform their own action.
